The knowledge base — OKF directory, agent-operated

One concept per markdown file with YAML frontmatter; markdown links form the knowledge graph. Agents draft and maintain; people confirm. Every agent-drafted entry carries a resource link back to its source record for provenance.

Capture instrument reference

Knowledge note (~15 minutes)

Triggers
Practitioner rotation; phase go/no-go boundary; any re-profiling trigger (sponsor change, Exception Review invoked, material scope/duration extension)
Owner
Delivery Owner (with the rotating practitioner where applicable)
Content
What we now know that we did not know before; what the next person on this engagement — or the next similar engagement — needs to be told
Fate
Consolidated by the closure retrospective, so closure becomes synthesis rather than the first capture

Closure retrospective (45-60 minutes)

Trigger
Gate 2 — every engagement, including early terminations
Owner
Practice Lead (owns), Delivery Owner (contributes)
Content
Went well / change / learned about client, technology, approach / commercial and scope issues / follow-on opportunities / reusable assets produced
Output
Delivery Knowledge Base entry (OKF type: Engagement Retrospective), with review_by date for the curation lifecycle

Reusable asset register

Captures
Accelerators, reference architectures, templates — created from work already done
Owner
Practice Lead per practice area
Signal
Asset-reuse per engagement: consulted or reused, logged at the Stage 1 transition meeting, reviewed at closure
Why
The single biggest gross-margin lever in technology professional services — estimation risk removed before the engagement starts

Demand-side metrics

Reuse
Entries referenced at Stage 1 per new engagement; reusable asset reuse rate; precedent exceptions consulted per Exception Review
Health
% of entries past their review_by date (staleness); knowledge note completion at mid-engagement events
Quality
Win Review knowledge-trail usefulness — useful, not just present
Floor
Retrospective and entry completion rates retained at 100%, demoted from target to floor
