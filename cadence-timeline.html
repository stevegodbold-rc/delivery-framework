Forum detail

Delivery Health Check Fortnightly

Leads
Delivery Owner
Attends
Practice Lead
Focus
Five health dimensions with written rationale; vehicle-specific evidence (EAC check for fixed-price, funding/PO runway for T&M); methodology artefacts consumed as evidence, not duplicated
Metrics
Health score (G/A/R by dimension), cost-to-complete/EAC variance, funding/PO runway, scope change velocity, unagreed scope drift
Outcomes
Risks surfaced before they are client-visible; Recovery and Exception triggers detected; Engagement Health Register entry logged

Client Business Review Monthly · per client

Leads
Delivery Owner
Attends
Client sponsor
Focus
Relationship health, delivery progress, risks, forward planning
Metrics
Completion rate (target 100%); stakeholder sentiment; relationship layers
Outcomes
Expectations aligned continuously rather than at crisis points; early relationship risk signals; expansion signals logged to CRM

Portfolio Health Review Monthly · anchor forum

Leads
Delivery Director (chair)
Attends
All Delivery Owners, Practice Lead, Resource Manager
Focus
Cross-portfolio health distribution, workforce pressure, concentration risk; WIP ceilings and capacity trade-offs; queue review (handoff, Recovery & Exception aging)
Metrics
Health distribution (% G/A/R), engagements in Recovery & Exception + days in status, engagement load vs. ceiling per Delivery Owner, handoff queue length, review completion rates
Outcomes
Explicit trade-off decisions at WIP ceilings; Project Board representation assigned; concentration risks named and owned

Capacity Review Monthly · two segments

Leads
Delivery Director
Attends
Resource Manager, Sales Lead, Practice Lead
Focus
Segment 1: demand reconciliation — weighted pipeline vs. available and planned capacity. Segment 2: workforce response — hiring lead times vs. forecast demand, skilling priorities, requisition queue
Metrics
Deal stage forecast, lead times close-to-start and advertise-to-ready (p50/p85), available/scheduled hours, remaining capacity vs. buffer, open requisitions vs. WIP limit, aged requisitions
Outcomes
Hiring decisions committed ahead of demand (advertise-to-ready lead time honoured); skilling investment prioritised; requisition limits held or explicitly raised

Commercial Health Review Monthly · three segments

Leads
Delivery Director + Finance Partner (co-owned)
Attends
Practice Lead (closure segment only)
Focus
Segment 1: forecast and bill-to-cash. Segment 2: retention schedule. Segment 3: closure health patterns
Metrics
Forecast-to-actual variance, revenue at risk (Amber/Red), DSO, milestone billings due 30 days, invoices unpaid beyond terms, retentions held and release rate, disputed final invoices, closure completion rates, early termination rate, days to final invoice
Outcomes
Revenue risk acted on before it lands in the accounts; retention follow-ups owned and dated; closure process patterns remediated

Workforce Review Monthly

Leads
Practice Lead + Resource Manager (co-owned; no Delivery Director)
Focus
Utilisation, bench and resource-gap queues, allocation gaps, skilling investment priorities, elasticity signals
Metrics
Billable utilisation (rolling 4-week), bench time by practitioner and bench queue as % of headcount, resource gap queue length and aging (>10 business days), skilling gaps identified vs. resolved
Outcomes
Allocation decisions made through conversation, not formula; aged gaps escalated; buffer sizing revisited when the bench outgrows Remaining Capacity

Delivery-Led Sales Review Monthly · CCO chair

Leads
Chief Customer Officer
Attends
Sales Lead; Delivery Director quarterly (contributor)
Focus
Relationship depth, scope evolution signals, expansion readiness across the active portfolio; account planning actions
Metrics
Active relationships by layer, stakeholder sentiment, follow-on opportunities in CRM (% of active engagements), client-initiated scope additions by delivery vehicle, declined/deferred requests
Outcomes
Delivery-sourced demand signals converted into pipeline; leakage from unlogged out-of-scope requests closed

Quarterly Calibration Block Quarterly · half-day

Leads
Delivery Director
Attends
Finance Partner, Sales Lead, Practice Lead, CCO — by segment
Focus
Segment 1: health-to-billing calibration. Segment 2: alignment health check, including deal quality correlation and estimate-vs-actual solution design variance on threshold deals. Segment 3: Win Review — 2-4 won deals walked end-to-end against the framework's own record (co-owned with CCO)
Metrics
% disputed invoices and extended-DSO engagements preceded by Amber/Red commercial health; revenue-at-risk probability weightings; T-shape density, core skill %, pipeline alignment %; Deal Quality at Handover aggregation and downstream correlation
Outcomes
Probability weightings recalibrated against evidence; structural sales/delivery drift surfaced; framework improvement opportunities fed to the annual design review; cross-boundary observations to sales and practice leadership

Exception Review As triggered

Leads
Delivery Director (convenes within 5 business days of notification)
Attends
Delivery Owner, Finance Partner, Practice Lead (where workforce implications)
Focus
Decisions on troubled engagements per the Delegation of Authority bands: concessions, scope renegotiation, write-offs, early termination. Fortnightly follow-up while open
Metrics
Trigger evidence: Red sustained two fortnights, EAC below threshold (fixed-price), funding/PO runway signal (T&M), or above-band decision required
Outcomes
Exception Decision Record; band-appropriate decision made by the right authority; alternate Gate 2 route invoked where termination or partial closure is agreed
Timing within each month is indicative — the framework fixes frequency and participation, not calendar position; fortnight placement here suggests a workable spread (portfolio and capacity early in the month feeding the commercial review later). The annual framework design review (Delivery Director, fed by Win Review outputs) falls outside this six-month window.
