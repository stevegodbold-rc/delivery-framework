Delivery Management Framework — Adoption Tiering

From minimum viable core to the full framework

Three adoption tiers keyed to organisational scale, per the second delivery-executive review. Each tier inherits everything below it; bold items are what the tier adds. The advancement gates between tiers are conditions, not dates — move when the evidence says the current tier is embedded, not when a quarter ends.

Tier 1 — Core

Minimum viable subset

~50 people · adopting cold · quarter one
~15metrics
1+1monthly forum + qtr block
1WIP limit

Stages & gates

  • Gate 1: transition meeting, Delivery Handover Record
  • Deal Quality score — Red commercial position notifies the Delivery Director day one
  • Gates 2-3, Closure Acknowledgement + escalation, retrospective, Finance Handover Record

Engagement control

  • Fortnightly health check with EAC / runway evidence checks — the self-rating antidote; non-negotiable
  • Recovery & Exception with Delegation of Authority bands
  • Monthly client business review

Portfolio

  • One merged monthly operating forum — same four chairs at this scale
  • Quarterly Calibration Block, kept sacrosanct
  • Delivery Owner load ceiling — the one WIP limit

Knowledge

  • Closure retrospectives + Stage 1 prior-knowledge check
  • Plain shared markdown folder — no agents, no conformance ambitions

Deliberately deferred

  • Win Review (needs a year of paper trail)
  • Account Delivery Record (until a second engagement exists in an account)
  • Delivery-led sales metric suite (log signals in CRM; skip measurement)
  • Full queue set beyond the DO ceiling
→advance gate A
Tier 2 — Operating

Full engagement machinery

~50-120 people · core embedded · year two
~35metrics
3+1monthly forums + qtr block
4managed queues

Everything in Core, plus:

Pre-sales interface

  • Pre-signature profiling on threshold deals; profiler becomes Delivery Owner
  • Solution design co-sign (Practice or Technical Lead)
  • Conditional acceptance at Gate 1; re-profiling triggers mid-flight
  • Deal Quality aggregation + downstream-outcome correlation — hold the line on the correlation, not the raw scores

Portfolio

  • Three monthly forums: Portfolio Health, Capacity, Commercial Health
  • Win Review joins the Calibration Block — paper trail now exists
  • Queues: handoff, resource gap, requisition WIP, bench %
  • Lead times as p50/p85 distributions

Accounts & growth

  • Account Delivery Record on multi-engagement accounts
  • Delivery-Led Sales Review, CCO-chaired

Knowledge

  • Knowledge notes at rotation, phase boundary, re-profiling
  • review_by curation lifecycle; demand-side metrics begin
  • OKF-shaped schema (Option A → B as volume justifies)
→advance gate B
Tier 3 — Scale

Full framework

~120-200 people · multiple practices
~65metrics
6managed queues
4KM agent roles

Everything in Operating, plus:

Accounts & authority

  • Account-aligned assignment; strategic-account definition live
  • Strategic-account health moderation (Practice Lead co-review, override visibility)
  • Full Delegation of Authority use, MD escalation band

Flow & forums

  • Full queue set with aging views; exception aging as governance signal
  • Methodology interfaces across a mixed portfolio (PRINCE2 boards, Scrum/Kanban evidence, phase-level triggers)
  • Full standing-forum structure with named segments

Knowledge

  • Agent-operated: enrichment, curation, retrieval, aggregation — requires a named tooling owner first
  • Reusable asset register with reuse-rate signal per practice

Prerequisites this tier exposes

  • Sub-portfolio construct — not yet designed
  • Plural-role forum design (multiple Practice Leads) — not yet designed
  • Named successors on strategic accounts — not yet designed

Advance gate ACore → Operating: move when…

  • The core cadence has held for two consecutive quarters without rationale-templating — written health rationales are still specific, not copy-paste
  • The first threshold deal or multi-engagement account has actually occurred
  • A year of closure paper trail exists to make the Win Review worth running
  • Delivery Owner framework load is measurably below the ~15-20% honesty ceiling

Advance gate BOperating → Scale: move when…

  • More than one practice area operates with its own Practice Lead — and the plural-role forum design exists to seat them
  • 10+ Delivery Owners; sub-portfolio construct designed before, not after, the Delivery Director's calendar breaks
  • A named tooling/platform owner exists for the knowledge agents — without one, consumption stays manual and must stay best-effort
  • Recurring revenue approaching ~25% of the book — which also makes the renewal gate (below) urgent, not optional

Not yet designed — known gaps at any tier

Named by the second executive review. These are not tier-3 luxuries; several become load-bearing the moment the conditions above are met.

Managed services renewal gateSurvived two reviews unbuilt. No renewal checkpoint, SLA health dimensions, or ARR/churn view — the account-growth engine has no renewal engine to convert into.
Sub-portfolio constructThe Delivery Director role is single-threaded; the position description's delegation note is an IOU, not a design.
Capacity contraction pathHiring is a managed queue; downsizing has no mechanism, forum, or owner. Flow is managed in one direction only.
Strategic-account delegation fixValue bands should apply within strategic accounts, MD informed rather than deciding below-band.
Formal dispute path & recovery plan artifactClient-initiated contractual disputes differ from mutual early termination; Red engagements need a dated, client-visible recovery plan.
Subcontractor / partner co-deliveryCommonly 10-30% of capacity at this scale; zero treatment in workforce, quality, or margin machinery.
Delivery Owner successionMid-engagement DO departure — especially on a Red engagement or strategic account — triggers nothing defined.
Project Board seats vs. WIPSenior Delivery Owners' board representation counts toward nobody's ceiling — invisible work the framework elsewhere forbids.
Tiering per the second delivery-executive review's verdict: full cold adoption at 50 people ≈ 15% two-quarter survival; subset adoption > 70%. Each tier's additions preserve the mechanisms the review rated worth defending (Recovery & Exception, evidence-forced health checks, methodology interfaces, flow management) while sequencing the weight.
